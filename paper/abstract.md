# Abstract

Tabular machine learning is traditionally dominated by gradient-boosted decision-tree algorithms, while tabular foundation models provide an alternative based on large-scale pretraining. This study compares TabPFN with XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost across different training-data regimes. The benchmark evaluates the four models on three binary tabular classification datasets: Adult, Bank Marketing, and Credit-G. Five random seeds and multiple training sizes are used to evaluate predictive performance and changes in model behaviour as training data increase. Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC are measured together with training and prediction time. The final benchmark contains 1,040 model-level observations. Results show that TabPFN provides the strongest aggregate performance on Bank Marketing, while CatBoost performs best across most metrics on Adult and Credit-G. Statistical analysis identifies 34 significant comparisons out of 45 at unadjusted p < 0.05, with 31 remaining significant after Holm correction. TabPFN also has substantially higher measured prediction times. Overall, the findings indicate that TabPFN is a complementary approach to gradient-boosted tree algorithms rather than a universal replacement.
