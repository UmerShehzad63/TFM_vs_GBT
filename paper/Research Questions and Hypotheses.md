# 2. Research Questions and Hypotheses

The study addresses the following primary research question:

> **How does TabPFN compare with established gradient-boosted tree algorithms across different training-data regimes in tabular classification?**

To address the primary question, four supporting research questions are examined:

1. **Does TabPFN provide an advantage when training data are limited?**

2. **How does the relative performance of TabPFN change as training size increases?**

3. **Is the performance advantage of TabPFN consistent across datasets and evaluation metrics?**

4. **What computational trade-offs exist between TabPFN and gradient-boosted tree models?**

Based on these research questions, four hypotheses were investigated:

### H1

**TabPFN will be competitive or superior to gradient-boosted tree models when training data are limited.**

### H2

**TabPFN's relative advantage will change as training size increases.**

### H3

**TabPFN's performance relative to tree-based models will vary across datasets and evaluation metrics.**

### H4

**Predictive advantages will be accompanied by computational trade-offs.**
